# Review cycle 2 — consolidated (10 reviewers, 2026-11-21; manuscript v2, adopted funny-euler/great-ride line)

Raw reviews: `REVIEW/cycle02_raw/R1..R10*.md`. Recommendations: 10 × Major revision. R9 (associate editor): **not
desk-rejected**; scope and format fit Advances in Applied Energy. Every reviewer reproduced the core result against
pooled CQR (window under-covered share 18.7 % → 0.4 %, site-cluster CI of difference [−24.1, −10.0] pp; R4). The issues
concern claims beyond the tables, tuning symmetry, the noise reference, the information set and data selection.

Tiers: **A** = re-analysis of existing results or text only; **B** = calibration-only recompute (trained models needed,
not in git → retrain first); **C** = data or pipeline change → full rerun.
