# R2 methodology — Major revision

Summary: code matches Eqs. (1)-(5) closely (scale, scores, interval, grid rounding, LOBO exclusion, GHCP weights, QShrink fallback verified). Remaining: (a) LOBO IS objective too flat to identify K and selects larger κ than reliability-optimal; (b) kNN prior harms first-week reliability, paper calls it neutral; (c) DP and stage-matching justifications don't fit implementation; (d) TQA-B uses target buildings. Headline numbers from the variant the paper advises against.

1. **Major — LOBO objective too flat, works against reliability.** main_BDG2_s0 tune_hsc_cqr at κ=336: 0.5957 (K=all), 0.5959 (K=25), 0.5956 (K=100); κ=∞ 0.6003 (only 0.8% worse) though under-coverage ~6x higher (21.7% vs 3.4%). K=all never selected for HSC-CQR in 15 runs (K 10-100). Target (sensitivity.csv, CQR, BDG2): K=all best at every κ (κ=336: 3.1% vs 3.2-3.5%); κ=96 gives 1.7% vs 3.1% at selected 336. Action: reliability-aware LOBO (calibration-building MACD / under-covered share / IS with coverage constraint) or fixed default κ; report flatness. Experiment: LOBO with MACD and under-covered share objectives on stored scores.
2. **Major — kNN prior harms week-1 coverage.** Random, α=0.1, week-1 mean coverage / share <0.85: BDG2 HSC-CQR 0.885/24.5%, −kNN 0.895/21.4%, CQR 0.892/23.8%; abs HSC 0.880/31.3% vs −kNN 0.921/14.2%. Table 5 HSC under-covered 9.2% vs −kNN 5.3%. Building-weighted κ=∞ prior worse than score-weighted CQR (21.7% vs 18.7%). Contradicts Sec 5.3, 6.2. Action: state localisation harmful early, explain (building-weighted averaging, descriptor); report week-1 for all ablations.
3. **Major — headline numbers from advised-against variant.** Abstract/conclusion = full HSC-CQR (pipeline.py:326-330); recommended simple 2.9%/1.1%; 0.437 ms for abs HSC with kNN (pipeline.py:318-321).
4. **Moderate — DP reading of Eq. (5) incorrect as stated.** DP predictive = (κF0 + Σδ)/(κ+n); +1 mass at +∞ is a conformal correction. Own scores pooled over stages 1..h−1 (calibration.py:206,209) vs stage-matched prior (197). κ limit discontinuous: finite-sample correction only at κ=∞ (201-204). Action: call it DP-motivated heuristic with conformal-style correction; state stage mismatch; fix limits sentence.
5. **Moderate — stage-matching explanation contradicted.** Sec 6.2 "comparable scale"; week-1 coverage SCP 0.863 vs SCP-stage 0.888 (BDG2), 0.884 vs 0.904 (UCI). Make claim score-specific; note own scores never stage-matched.
6. **Moderate — kNN descriptor dominated by load shape.** features.py:137: 24 profile + 1 variability + 1 log-load, standardised Euclidean (calibration.py:133-136); variability ~1/26 of distance, 0 at h=1 (features.py:136). Qualify claim or test variability/log-load or own-score-quantile descriptor.
7. **Minor — TQA-B λ uses target buildings.** calibration.py:322-327 λ=(α−0.01)/max over target buildings of g; contradicts Sec 3.4/4.3. Use calibration ranks or theoretical max N/(N+1).
8. **Minor — κ=0 degenerate; γ=0 not exactly pooled.** κ=0 at h=1 gives 500·s (objective ~18.5); aci_quantiles (228-229) uses 1−α without ceil((1−α)(N+1))/N correction.
