# Results {#sec:results}

All shares in this section use one unit, the building: a building's metrics are averaged over the seeds in which it was a target. "Under-covered" means coverage below 0.85 at the 90 % target, evaluated over the 56-day window, in the first week, or in each week of weeks 2–8. The *oracle reference* is the share that perfectly calibrated *static* intervals with the building's own day-to-day error dependence would show (Section [-@sec:setup]); it is the same for every method.

## Pooled calibration leaves many new buildings unreliable

The global LightGBM forecaster reaches a median CV(RMSE) of {{point_BDG2_cvrmse_lgb}} on new BDG2 buildings over their first 56 forecast days (seasonal naive: {{point_BDG2_cvrmse_naive}}; MLP: {{point_BDG2_cvrmse_mlp}}) and {{point_UCI_cvrmse_lgb}} on UCI clients (naive {{point_UCI_cvrmse_naive}}; MLP {{point_UCI_cvrmse_mlp}}). Its pooled conformal intervals are well calibrated on average ({{main_BDG2_CQR_mean_cov}} mean coverage on BDG2) but not building by building (Table [-@tbl:main]). Pooled CQR leaves {{tt_BDG2_CQR_under}} of the BDG2 buildings and {{tt_UCI_CQR_under}} of the UCI clients under-covered over the window, against an oracle reference of {{tt_BDG2_ref_win}} and {{tt_UCI_ref_win}}, and in an average week of weeks 2–8 it leaves {{tt_BDG2_CQR_wk_late}} and {{tt_UCI_CQR_wk_late}} under-covered (oracle {{tt_BDG2_ref_week}} and {{tt_UCI_ref_week}}). Over eight weeks, {{tt_BDG2_CQR_any80}} of the BDG2 buildings have at least one week below 0.80. Under-coverage is concentrated in hard-to-forecast buildings (Spearman correlation of coverage with CV(RMSE): {{ea_BDG2_rho_CQR}}), and it is not a borderline effect: {{th_BDG2_CQR_under_5pt}} of the BDG2 buildings miss the target by more than five points (Fig. [-@fig:cdf]). Direct probabilistic models do not solve the problem (MLP-QR: {{main_BDG2_MLPQR_under_rate}} under-covered on BDG2; the Gaussian MLP lowers this to {{main_BDG2_MLPGauss_under_rate}} only by over-covering on average at a median interval score of {{main_BDG2_MLPGauss_rel_IS}} instead of {{main_BDG2_CQR_rel_IS}}). A uniform level offset between calibration and target periods explains only {{off_BDG2_drop}} points of the pooled excess; the rest is heterogeneity between buildings.

## Week-by-week reliability with two-timescale calibration {#sec:weekly}

TTSC removes most of this unreliability at every time scale (Table [-@tbl:main], Fig. [-@fig:ttsc]). Over the window it leaves {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_under}} (BDG2) and {{tt_UCI_TTSC_under}} (UCI) of the buildings under-covered. In the first week, {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_wk1}} and {{tt_UCI_TTSC_wk1}} are under-covered, against {{tt_BDG2_CQR_wk1}} and {{tt_UCI_CQR_wk1}} with pooled CQR. In weeks 2–8, the share of under-covered buildings per week falls to {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_wk_late}} on BDG2 and {{tt_UCI_TTSC_wk_late}} on UCI, *below* the oracle reference of {{tt_BDG2_ref_week}} and {{tt_UCI_ref_week}}, and the share of buildings with any week below 0.80 falls from {{tt_BDG2_CQR_any80}} to {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_any80}} (BDG2) and from {{tt_UCI_CQR_any80}} to {{tt_UCI_TTSC_any80}} (UCI). Static calibration cannot get below the oracle reference, because even perfectly calibrated static intervals inherit the week-to-week variation of a building's errors. TTSC can, because its fast component widens the interval in a week in which the building misses and narrows it in a week in which it over-covers.

The improvement over the strongest single-timescale alternatives is large and consistent (Supplementary Table S23). Against shrinkage alone, TTSC lowers the weekly under-covered share in weeks 2–8 by {{tc_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late}} percentage points on BDG2 (95 % site-cluster bootstrap CI {{tc_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late_ci}}; Holm-adjusted {{tc_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late_p}}) and by {{tc_UCI_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late}} points on UCI (CI {{tc_UCI_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late_ci}}), and the share of buildings with any week below 0.80 by {{tc_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_any80}} and {{tc_UCI_HSCCQR_recency_capped_any80}} points. Against ACI tuned by the same rule, the reductions are {{tc_BDG2_ACICQR_cov_wk_late}} and {{tc_UCI_ACICQR_cov_wk_late}} points in weeks 2–8 and {{tc_BDG2_ACICQR_cov_wk1}} and {{tc_UCI_ACICQR_cov_wk1}} points in the first week. Every comparison in the declared family (TTSC versus shrinkage, quantile shrinkage, ACI and pooled CQR on weekly under-coverage, any week below 0.80 and failed weekly checks, both datasets) is significant after Holm adjustment.

*Held-out confirmation.* TTSC was designed after inspecting seeds 0 and 1 of BDG2 and seed 0 of UCI. On seeds 2–4, which played no role in its design, the results are the same: weekly under-coverage in weeks 2–8 of {{tt_BDG2h_TTSC_wk_late}} versus {{tt_BDG2h_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late}} for shrinkage alone and {{tt_BDG2h_CQR_wk_late}} for pooled CQR on BDG2, and {{tt_UCIh_TTSC_wk_late}} versus {{tt_UCIh_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late}} and {{tt_UCIh_CQR_wk_late}} on UCI (Table [-@tbl:main]).

*Cost in sharpness.* The fast component spends some width: the median relative interval score of TTSC is {{tc_BDG2_CQR_log_rel_IS}} above that of pooled CQR on BDG2 (CI {{tc_BDG2_CQR_log_rel_IS_ci}}) and {{tc_UCI_CQR_log_rel_IS}} on UCI (CI {{tc_UCI_CQR_log_rel_IS_ci}}), while the mean interval score changes by {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_rel_IS_mean_vs_cqr}} and {{tt_UCI_TTSC_rel_IS_mean_vs_cqr}}. The gain $\gamma$, selected on calibration buildings, was {{tp_gamma_main}} in the main runs; it reached the edge of its grid in {{tp_gamma_edge_share}} of all runs.

## Why two timescales {#sec:ablation}

Each component alone fails where the other succeeds (Table [-@tbl:main], Fig. [-@fig:ttsc]). *Shrinkage alone* ($\gamma=0$) provides the informed start: it brings first-week under-coverage to {{tt_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk1}} on BDG2 and window under-coverage to {{tt_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_under}}, but in weeks 2–8 it stays at {{tt_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_wk_late}}, above the oracle reference, because a distributional estimate follows within-week changes only slowly. *Feedback alone* (ACI on the pooled distribution) has no informed start: from the pooled radius, binary misses are too weak a signal to find a building's level quickly, so it leaves {{tt_BDG2_ACICQR_cov_wk1}} under-covered in the first week and {{tt_BDG2_ACICQR_cov_under}} over the window. *Cumulative* instead of windowed feedback, with the same gain, keeps old disturbances in the level and is less effective in weeks 2–8 ({{tt_BDG2_TTSC_cumulative_feedback_wk_late}} versus {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_wk_late}}). Using the building's own data one day later (for gate closures before the end of the previous day) costs part of the gain ({{tt_BDG2_TTSC_delay_1d_wk_late}} in weeks 2–8, {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_delay_1d_wk1}} in week 1) but remains better than shrinkage alone. Without the cap, the fast component occasionally produces very wide intervals (median interval score {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_uncapped_rel_IS_median}} versus {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_rel_IS_median}}), so the cap is essential.

The slow component itself was studied in detail (Supplementary Section S5). Shrinkage of the calibration towards the building's own scores is a property of a family rather than of one estimator: quantile shrinkage performs almost as well as score shrinkage over the window and in the first week, and both remove nearly all window-level excess ({{x_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_under}} and {{x_BDG2_QShrinkCQR_cov_under}} on BDG2). The first-week gain comes from a small pseudo-count ({{mk_BDG2_under_wk1_rho0.95_k48}} with and {{mk_BDG2_under_wk1_rho1.0_k48}} without recency at $\kappa$ = 48 h), recency acts later in the window, and similarity-localised priors, onboarding-stage matching and stage-wise studentisation do not help (Fig. [-@fig:ablation]). Over the reliability–sharpness frontier of each family (Fig. [-@fig:frontier]), ACI reaches window-level reliability only in a narrow band of step sizes and never repairs the first week without a large loss of sharpness ({{fr_BDG2_ACI_0.005_ISdelta}} median interval score at $\gamma$ = 0.005, {{fr_BDG2_ACI_0.01_ISdelta}} at 0.01), which is why feedback needs the informed start that shrinkage provides.

## Generalisation and stress tests

Table [-@tbl:robust] repeats the comparison under every other protocol. In {{to_n_ttsc_lowest_weekly}} of {{to_n_conditions}} conditions, TTSC has the lowest weekly under-covered share in weeks 2–8 among all strategies, including on campuses that contributed no training or calibration building ({{to_lso_BDG2_TTSC_wk_late}} versus {{to_lso_BDG2_CQR_wk_late}} for pooled CQR), after transfer from BDG2 to Portuguese UCI clients ({{to_xds:BDG2toUCI_UCI_TTSC_wk_late}} versus {{to_xds:BDG2toUCI_UCI_CQR_wk_late}}) and in the much harder opposite direction ({{to_xds:UCItoBDG2_BDG2_TTSC_wk_late}} versus {{to_xds:UCItoBDG2_BDG2_CQR_wk_late}}), at nominal 95 % and 80 %, under meter noise, spikes, missing data, outages, level shifts, ramps, small calibration pools and a weak forecaster. Meter noise on the new buildings, which breaks pooled calibration from clean calibration buildings ({{to_noise15_BDG2_CQR_wk_late}} under-covered per week at 15 % noise), is absorbed by both timescales ({{to_noise15_BDG2_TTSC_wk_late}}). Two stresses remain hard for every strategy: a level shift in the first two weeks keeps first-week under-coverage near 40 % for all methods, and after a level increase of 40 % at day 28 TTSC only reaches the oracle reference ({{to_shiftp40_BDG2_TTSC_wk_late}} versus {{to_shiftp40_BDG2_TTSC_ref_week}}). Across conditions, the median interval score of TTSC lies between {{to_isdelta_min}} and {{to_isdelta_max}} of pooled CQR's.

## Operational case: weekly-settled capacity-headroom offers {#sec:operational}

Consider an aggregator that offers, for every enrolled building and hour, day-ahead capacity headroom below a contracted limit, planned on the upper interval bound, and whose performance is checked per building and week: a week fails if the realised load exceeds the planned upper bound in more than 10 % of the hours, twice the nominal 5 %. With pooled CQR, {{tt_BDG2_CQR_breach_wk}} of the building-weeks fail this check on BDG2 and {{tt_UCI_CQR_breach_wk}} on UCI. TTSC reduces this to {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_breach_wk}} and {{tt_UCI_TTSC_breach_wk}} (paired differences {{tc_BDG2_CQR_breach_wk}} and {{tc_UCI_CQR_breach_wk}} points, CIs {{tc_BDG2_CQR_breach_wk_ci}} and {{tc_UCI_CQR_breach_wk_ci}}), and below shrinkage alone ({{tt_BDG2_HSCCQR_recency_capped_breach_wk}}) and ACI ({{tt_BDG2_ACICQR_cov_breach_wk}}). The planning cost, the planned upper bound plus a penalty proportional to violations (Eq. [-@eq:cost]), changes by {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_cost10_vs_cqr}}, {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_cost20_vs_cqr}} and {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_cost40_vs_cqr}} on BDG2 for penalty ratios of 10, 20 and 40 (UCI: {{tt_UCI_TTSC_cost10_vs_cqr}}, {{tt_UCI_TTSC_cost20_vs_cqr}}, {{tt_UCI_TTSC_cost40_vs_cqr}}). The average headroom cost is therefore essentially unchanged; TTSC moves the planning margin to the buildings and weeks that need it, so that fewer weekly performance checks fail. The value of this depends on how failed checks are penalised. Under a purely linear, portfolio-level penalty it is small; where failed weekly checks carry a fixed charge, a reputational cost or exclusion from the programme, it is the relevant improvement.

## Computational cost

The online step of TTSC (binning the new scores, updating the decayed counts and the windowed level, and reading one mixture quantile) takes well under a millisecond per building-day on one CPU core ({{time_BDG2_recency_apply_ms}} ms for the shrinkage step, against {{time_BDG2_lgb_infer_ms}} ms for the LightGBM inference itself). The state per building is one count vector of {{time_BDG2_state_KB}} kB plus seven daily miss counts; leave-one-building-out selection of both parameters takes seconds on a single core. Cost per building-day is constant in the number of buildings and in onboarding time.

## Where the calibration still fails {#sec:errors}

Even with TTSC, single weeks of under-coverage remain common: {{tt_BDG2_TTSC_any85}} of the BDG2 buildings and {{tt_UCI_TTSC_any85}} of the UCI clients have at least one week below 0.85 over eight weeks (pooled CQR: {{tt_BDG2_CQR_any85}} and {{tt_UCI_CQR_any85}}; static oracle: {{tt_BDG2_ref_any}} and {{tt_UCI_ref_any}}). Occasional weekly misses are therefore unavoidable with intervals of this sharpness, and contracts should tolerate them; what TTSC removes is their concentration in the same buildings week after week. For the slow component, the remaining failures were analysed in detail. {{ERROR_ANALYSIS_TEXT}} Fig. [-@fig:examples] shows the intervals for two pre-specified buildings: the one at the 10th percentile and the one at the median of per-building CQR coverage.
