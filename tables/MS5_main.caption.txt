Week-by-week reliability of new buildings (random-building protocol, nominal 90 %; unit = building, averaged over the seeds in which it is a target). Window, Week 1, Weeks 2–8: share of buildings with coverage < 0.85 over the 56-day window, in the first week, and per week in weeks 2–8. Any week < 0.80: share of buildings with at least one week below 0.80. Breach weeks: share of building-weeks in which the upper bound is exceeded in more than 10 % of hours (twice the nominal 5 %), i.e. a failed weekly performance check of a headroom contract. Rel. IS: interval score / mean load. Cost Δ: change of the mean capacity-headroom cost (Eq. 9, ρ_p = 20) versus pooled CQR. Held-out: seeds 2–4, not inspected while TTSC was designed. Oracle reference: perfectly calibrated static intervals with the observed serial dependence of misses (block bootstrap). All strategies use the same LightGBM quantile models and CQR scores and are tuned by leave-one-building-out on calibration buildings.
