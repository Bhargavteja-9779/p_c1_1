Paired per-building comparison of PCS-CQR with each strategy (random-building protocol, nominal 90 %). CD = |coverage − 0.9|; Under = under-covered indicator (percentage points); IS = relative interval score. Diff. = PCS-CQR minus baseline, building-weighted mean (negative = PCS-CQR better); 95 % CI by site-cluster bootstrap (BDG2, 10 sites) or building bootstrap (UCI); p = two-sided sign-flip randomisation test with whole sites flipped (BDG2; exact, 1024 patterns, so p ≥ 0.002) or buildings flipped (UCI), Holm-adjusted within dataset, family and metric.
