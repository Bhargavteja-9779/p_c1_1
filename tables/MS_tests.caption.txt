Paired per-building comparison of HSC-CQR with each baseline (random-building protocol, nominal 90 %). CD = absolute coverage deviation |cov_b − 0.9|; IS = relative interval score. Mean diff. = HSC-CQR minus baseline, averaged over buildings (negative = HSC-CQR better); 95 % CI by site-cluster bootstrap (BDG2) or building bootstrap (UCI); Better = share of buildings for which HSC-CQR is better; r_rb = matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation (positive = HSC-CQR better); p = two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank, Holm-adjusted within dataset and metric.
