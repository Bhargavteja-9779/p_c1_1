Per-building reliability and sharpness of the calibration strategies (random-building protocol, nominal 90 %, 5 seeds; all strategies except MLP-QR/MLP-Gauss use the same LightGBM quantile models and CQR scores). Coverage: mean per-building coverage over the 56-day onboarding window; Under-cov.: share of buildings with coverage < 0.85 (over the window and in the first week); P10: 10th percentile of per-building coverage; Under-prot.: share of buildings whose upper bound is exceeded in more than 10 % of hours (nominal 5 %); Rel. IS: median over buildings of the interval score divided by the building's mean load. Mean ± s.d. over seeds. '(cov)': parameter selected with the per-building coverage objective.
