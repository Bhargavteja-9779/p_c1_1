Per-building interval reliability and sharpness on UCI (random-building protocol, nominal 90%, 0 seeds, ≈0 target buildings per seed). MACD: mean |coverage − 0.90| over buildings (coverage over the 56-day onboarding window); Under-cov.: share of buildings with coverage below 0.85 (wk 1: first 7 forecast days only); P10: 10th percentile of per-building coverage; Rel. width and Rel. IS: medians over buildings of width and interval score divided by the building's mean load; Booking failures: share of buildings whose upper bound is exceeded in more than 10 % of hours (twice the nominal 5.0 %), e.g. a capacity reservation sized by the upper bound; Reserve cost: capacity-booking cost of the upper bound (pinball loss at 0.950) in % of mean load. Mean ± s.d. over seeds. HSC-CQR = the pre-registered configuration (similarity prior, stage matching, κ tuned on the interval score).
