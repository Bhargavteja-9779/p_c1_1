Paired per-building comparison of HSC-CQR recency with the strongest alternatives (random-building protocol, 90 %). CD = |cov_b − 0.9| over the window; CD wk 1 = the same for the first week; IS = relative interval score. Mean diff. = HSC-CQR recency minus baseline (negative = HSC-CQR recency better); Better = share of buildings for which HSC-CQR recency is better; r_rb = matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation (positive = better); p (Holm) = two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank over buildings, Holm-adjusted within dataset, metric and family; p (sites) = Wilcoxon on site-mean differences (BDG2, 10 sites; unadjusted; '–' for UCI, a single site).
