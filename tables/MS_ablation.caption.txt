Component ablation (random-building protocol, 90 %, mean ± s.d. over 5 seeds). '-stage': prior pooled over all onboarding stages; '-kNN': prior from all calibration buildings (K = all); 'simple': both removed; '-shrink': prior only (κ = ∞); 'fixed-κ': simple variant with κ = 168 h (one week) instead of the leave-one-building-out choice (a donor–local mixture with fixed pseudo-count); Self-*: κ = 0 with fallback to the pooled quantile. Except for fixed-κ, every variant is re-tuned on calibration buildings with the component removed.
