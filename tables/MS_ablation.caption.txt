Design elements (random-building protocol, 90 %, mean ± s.d. over 5 seeds). PCS-CQR: uniform prior over all calibration buildings, no stage matching, κ by leave-one-building-out (LOBO) on the reliability objective (minimum coverage deviation among κ within 2 % of the best LOBO interval score). κ=168: fixed one-week pseudo-count (donor–local mixture); IS-tuned: κ by LOBO on the interval score (pre-registered objective); site-grouped: LOBO leaving out the whole site; +stage / +kNN: stage-matched / similarity-localised prior (re-tuned); HSC-CQR: pre-registered configuration (kNN + stage, IS-tuned); -shrink: prior only (κ = ∞); Self-*: κ = 0 with pooled fallback. QShrink: quantile-level shrinkage (λ by LOBO-IS, by the reliability objective, or fixed at 168). Lower block: the same on the absolute-residual score.
