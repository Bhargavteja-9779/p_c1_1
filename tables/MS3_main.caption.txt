Per-building reliability and sharpness (random-building protocol, nominal 90 %; unit = building, metrics averaged over the seeds in which it is a target: 837 BDG2 buildings, 270 UCI clients). Under-cov.: share of buildings with coverage < 0.85 over the 56-day window, in the first week, and per week in weeks 2–8 (mean over weeks); Any wk < 0.80: share with at least one week below 0.80. Oracle reference: share expected for a building that is perfectly calibrated but has the observed serial dependence of misses (7-day moving-block bootstrap of miss indicators re-centred at 0.90; method-independent). Rel. IS: interval score divided by the building's mean load (median and mean over buildings). Width ratio: interval width relative to pooled CQR for the same building and seed (99th percentile / maximum). All strategies use the same LightGBM quantile models and CQR scores; '(cov)': parameter selected by the constrained leave-one-building-out objective; 'delay 1d': own scores used one day later.
