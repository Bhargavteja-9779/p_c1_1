Stress tests on BDG2 (3 seeds; unit = building). Columns 2–5: share of buildings (%) with coverage < 0.85 over the 56-day window (HSC-CQR recency: mean ± s.d. over seeds, the s.d. reflecting split variability); * = paired McNemar test against HSC-CQR recency, p < 0.05. Wk 1: under-covered share in the first week. Under-prot.: share of buildings whose upper bound is exceeded in more than 10 % of hours (nominal 5 %). Perturbations are applied to the target buildings only.
