Per-building interval reliability and sharpness on BDG2 (random-building protocol, nominal 90 %, 5 seeds, ≈301 target buildings per seed). Coverage: mean per-building coverage over the 56-day onboarding window; wk 1: first 7 forecast days; MACD: mean |coverage − 0.90|; Under-cov.: share of buildings with coverage < 0.85; P10: 10th percentile of per-building coverage; Rel. width and Rel. IS: medians over buildings of width and interval score divided by the building's mean load. Mean ± s.d. over seeds.
